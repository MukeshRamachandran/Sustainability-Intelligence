KCT Climate & Sustainability Observatory

About This Dashboard

The KCT Climate & Sustainability Observatory presents a data-driven overview of the environmental performance of Kumaraguru College of Technology. The dashboard monitors key indicators such as:

  • Greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions
  • Energy and water usage
  • Waste management
  • Green cover maintenance
  • Community-focused environmental education

These insights help strategize institutional sustainability initiatives and guide Kumaraguru Institutions toward achieving long-term environmental goals.

Data Sources

The data presented on this platform is collected from various operational departments of the college, including:

Source Description
Fleet Fuel Usage Petrol and diesel consumed by college vehicles
Diesel Generators Fuel used in backup DG sets
Grid Electricity Power drawn from the electricity grid
Renewable Energy On-campus renewable generation (solar, wind, etc.)
Water Consumption Campus-wide water usage records
Waste Generation & Diversion Waste produced and diverted from landfills
Field Surveys Total green cover and community outreach impact assessments

All collected data is transformed into a series of visual representations, helping users explore KCT's sustainability efforts and understand how the institution is progressing toward a more sustainable future.

Carbon Footprint Calculation

The Greenhouse Gas (GHG) emissions inventory for Kumaraguru Institutions has been developed in accordance with ISO 14064-1:2018 and the GHG Protocol, using an activity-based quantification approach. Emissions are calculated by multiplying activity-based data with appropriate emission factors derived from national and international sources.

CO₂ Emissions = Σ (Activity Data × Emission Factor)

Glossary of Terms

Emissions Scopes

Term Definition
Scope 1 Emissions Direct emissions produced by activities controlled by the institution. In this dashboard, Scope 1 mainly includes fuel used for campus Fleet and diesel used in generators. For example, petrol and diesel consumed by college vehicles and diesel used in DG sets are converted into CO₂e emissions using standard emission factors — helping identify the direct carbon impact created by Fleet and backup power systems.
Scope 2 Emissions Indirect emissions from purchased electricity. The college uses electricity from the grid for academic blocks, administrative buildings, labs, and other facilities. Even though these emissions are not produced directly on campus, grid electricity carries an associated carbon impact because it is generated from various energy sources, including coal.

Core Concepts

Term Definition
Activity Data A quantitative measure of an activity that results in a GHG emission or removal over a specific period. Examples: liters of fuel consumed, kilowatt-hours (kWh) of electricity used, distance traveled in kilometers, or mass of waste generated.
Renewable Energy Energy derived from natural, non-fossil sources that replenish faster than they are consumed. Examples: wind, solar, hydro, geothermal, ocean/tidal, and sustainably sourced biomass.
Gross GHG Emissions The total amount of greenhouse gases released into the atmosphere from all sources within defined boundaries, calculated before accounting for any GHG removals, carbon offsets, or credits.
Net GHG Emissions The net sum of total gross GHG emissions minus any verified GHG removals or reductions.
Net Emissions = Gross Emissions − GHG Removals/Reductions
