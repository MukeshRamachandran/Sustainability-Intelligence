About This Dashboard

The K-COSMOS (Kumaraguru Climate Observatory and Sustainability Monitoring System) presents a data-driven overview of the environmental performance of Kumaraguru College of Technology. The dashboard monitors key indicators such as:

  • Greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions
  • Energy and water usage
  • Waste management
  • Green cover maintenance
  • Community-focused environmental education

These insights help strategize institutional sustainability initiatives and guide Kumaraguru Institutions toward achieving long-term environmental goals.

Data Sources

The data presented on this platform is collected from various operational departments of the college, including:

Source Description
Fleet Fuel Usage Petrol and diesel consumed by college vehicles
Diesel Generators Fuel used in backup DG sets
Grid Electricity Power drawn from the electricity grid
Renewable Energy On-campus renewable generation (solar, wind, etc.)
Water Consumption Campus-wide water usage records
Waste Generation & Diversion Waste produced and diverted from landfills
Field Surveys Total green cover and community outreach impact assessments

All collected data is transformed into a series of visual representations, helping users explore Kumaraguru Institution's sustainability efforts and understand how the institution is progressing toward a more sustainable future.

Operational GHG Calculation

A Phase 1.4 public release reports a bounded operational indicator derived by the backend from frozen, governed calculation results. Scope 1 includes Petrol, Transport Diesel, DG Diesel and LPG CO₂e; Scope 2 is grid electricity CO₂e. The combined value is not a complete Scope 3 or universal institutional GHG inventory. Estimated avoided grid emissions are reported separately, not subtracted from Operational GHG. Waste emissions and Green Cover carbon sequestration are outside the current inventory boundary. Earlier immutable releases may not contain the Phase 1.4 indicators and will show those values as unavailable.

Operational GHG = Scope 1 + Scope 2

Glossary of Terms

Emissions Scopes

Term Definition
Scope 1 Emissions In this public contract, Scope 1 is the published sum of Petrol CO₂e, Transport Diesel CO₂e, DG Diesel CO₂e and LPG CO₂e. If any required frozen component result is missing, Scope 1 is unavailable.
Scope 2 Emissions In this public contract, Scope 2 is the frozen published CO₂e result for grid electricity.

Core Concepts

Term Definition
Activity Data A quantitative measure of an activity that results in a GHG emission or removal over a specific period. Examples: liters of fuel consumed, kilowatt-hours (kWh) of electricity used, distance traveled in kilometers, or mass of waste generated.
Renewable Energy Energy derived from natural, non-fossil sources that replenish faster than they are consumed. Examples: wind, solar, hydro, geothermal, ocean/tidal, and sustainably sourced biomass.
Operational GHG Emissions The published Scope 1 + Scope 2 result for the reporting period. It is not presented as a complete institutional footprint.
Operational GHG per capita The backend-published Operational GHG result converted to kilograms and divided by the governed population reference for that reporting year.
