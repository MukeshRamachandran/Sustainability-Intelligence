Introduction

Kumaraguru Institutions is a multi-campus educational institution in Tamil Nadu, South India, offering undergraduate, postgraduate, and research programs in Engineering, Technology, Management, Agriculture, Liberal Arts, and Science. Guided by its motto, "Character is Life," the institution is committed to academic excellence, holistic development, and nurturing responsible individuals with integrity and purpose. Through experiential learning, innovative research, and strong community engagement, Kumaraguru fosters a culture of inquiry and social responsibility, empowering students to address real-world challenges and contribute meaningfully to society.

Microcosm is a long-term, campus-wide environmental sustainability initiative at Kumaraguru Institutions that aims to transform the institution into a model of ecological balance, resource efficiency, and responsible stewardship.

This Carbon Emissions Report provides a transparent account of our Greenhouse Gas (GHG) inventory across energy, transport, and waste. By quantifying these operational impacts, we establish a robust baseline for targeted reduction strategies and reaffirm our continuous commitment to environmental sustainability.
