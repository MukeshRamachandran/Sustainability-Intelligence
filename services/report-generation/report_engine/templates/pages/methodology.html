Methodology & Conclusion

Reporting Standards and Final Insights

Purpose of the Report

This report presents the Greenhouse Gas (GHG) emissions inventory for Kumaraguru Institutions for the reporting period, compiled in accordance with ISO 14064-1:2018. The objective of this inventory is to measure and disclose the institution's GHG emissions, and provide reliable data to support future emissions reduction strategies.

GHG Quantification Approach

The inventory has been developed using an activity-based quantification approach. Emissions were calculated by multiplying activity data (e.g., fuel consumption, electricity consumption) with appropriate emission factors derived from recognized national and international sources (IPCC, CEA, DEFRA).

Organizational Boundary

The organizational boundary has been defined using the operational control approach. All facilities and operations over which Kumaraguru Institutions has operational control are included. Scope 3 emissions have been assessed at a screening level but are excluded from quantitative reporting due to the unavailability of consolidated data.

Conclusion

This GHG inventory enhances the understanding of emission sources within campus operations and provides a foundation for tracking environmental performance. The institution intends to progressively improve data quality, expand emission coverage, and integrate climate considerations into operational decision-making.
